Your Safety Matters

Built to protect students from scams

As an academic prototype, BoligMatch cannot verify properties in person. Instead, we've built multiple layers of scam-reduction features so you can browse and connect with confidence.

✉️

Email Verification

All users must verify their email. Student emails increase trust and reduce fake accounts.

🚩

Report & Flag System

Report suspicious listings or users. Flagged accounts are reviewed and removed if necessary.

💬

In-Platform Messaging

All communication stays on BoligMatch. Messages are monitored for scam keywords automatically.

⚠️

Safety Warnings & Tips

First-time users see clear warnings: never pay before viewing, avoid wire transfers, always meet in person.
