How BoligMatch
works

A peer-to-peer platform connecting international students with room owners in Denmark. No waiting lists, no subscriptions, no hidden costs.

🎓 For International Students
Find a room in four steps
BoligMatch is designed for students arriving in Denmark — no CPR number required to start, no subscription fee, no waiting list.
✉️ 1
Create a free account
Register with your email. Choose the "Student" role. Verify your email in one click. No subscription required — ever.
🔍 2
Search & filter rooms
Filter by city, maximum monthly rent, and maximum deposit. See only rooms you can actually afford — unique to BoligMatch.
💬 3
Message the owner directly
Send a message through the platform. No phone numbers or external apps needed. All conversations are stored safely.
🏠 4
View, sign, move in
Arrange an in-person viewing. Sign a written contract. Pay the deposit only after viewing. Then move in on your terms.
💰
Filter by deposit amount
Standard Danish apartments require up to 3 months deposit plus prepaid rent — often DKK 15,000–25,000 upfront. BoligMatch lets you filter listings to only show rooms with 1-month deposits, making it genuinely accessible for students arriving with limited savings.
🛡️
Built-in scam protection
Every message is monitored for known scam phrases (Western Union, wire transfer, gift cards). Suspicious listings are flagged for admin review. Safety warnings appear throughout the messaging flow to keep you informed and protected.
📍
Cities covered
Listings currently cover Copenhagen, Aarhus, Aalborg, and Odense — Denmark's four major student cities. Each city has local neighbourhoods listed, so you can search close to your campus or university.
❤️
Save rooms for later
Found a room you like but not ready to commit? Save it with the heart button and it will appear in your personal saved rooms list. Compare multiple rooms side by side in your own time.
🏠 For Room Owners
List your room in four steps
BoligMatch is completely free for owners. Post your room, manage enquiries, and choose your tenant — all in one place.
📝 1
Register as an owner
Create a free account with the "Owner" role. Verify your email address. No subscription, no listing fees — ever.
📸 2
Create your listing
Fill in the room details, upload photos (minimum 3), and set your rent and deposit clearly. Go live in under 5 minutes.
📩 3
Receive student enquiries
Students message you directly through the platform. Reply from your dashboard inbox. All conversations are stored and safe.
🤝 4
Choose your tenant
Arrange a viewing, select the right student, provide a written contract, and collect the deposit. Full control at every stage.
🆓
Always free to list
Unlike BoligPortal which charges around DKK 399 per month, BoligMatch is and always will be free for room owners. There are no listing fees, no commission, and no subscription. The platform is funded as an academic project.
🎓
Reach motivated students
BoligMatch is used specifically by international students who are actively looking for rooms and ready to move quickly. Unlike Facebook groups, listings here come with filtering tools so your listing reaches the right people.
⚙️
Full listing management
Edit or remove your listing at any time from your dashboard. Mark it as unavailable when rented. Listings auto-expire after 90 days so the platform stays fresh. Track how many students have viewed your listing.
📋
Provide a written contract
Under the Danish Rent Act (Lejeloven), tenants are entitled to a written rental contract. BoligMatch reminds owners of this throughout the listing process. This protects both parties and ensures the arrangement is legally sound.
